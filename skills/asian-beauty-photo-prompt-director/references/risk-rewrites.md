# Risk Rewrites

Use this reference before final prompt generation whenever user wording implies sensuality, body emphasis, private scenes, or platform-sensitive content.

## Red-Line Requests

Refuse and offer a safe portrait alternative when the request includes:

- minors, teen framing, school-uniform sexual framing, loli, childlike sexual appeal
- nudity, full nudity, exposed private parts, explicit sexual acts
- private-part close-ups, fetish framing, pornographic posing
- coercive, non-consensual, or exploitative sexual framing

## Rewrite Levels

| Level | User intent | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| L1 temperament | gaze, mood, expression | convert to emotion, lighting, gaze, and composition |
| L2 figure/fashion | silhouette, curves, fitted clothing | convert to fashion styling and whole-body silhouette language |
| L3 scene | bedroom, bathroom, low angle, reclined pose | convert to commercial editorial or luxury brand scene language |

## Content Levels

| Command | Strategy |
|---|---|
| `/safe` | Conservative commercial portrait. Remove sensual wording, use tasteful clothing, eye-level or slightly high-angle camera, studio/street/garden/spa-safe scene. |
| `/tasteful` | Allow refined attraction through gaze, styling, fabric, and lighting. Keep clothing tasteful and non-explicit. |
| `/editorial-heat` | High-fashion magazine heat: body-skimming silhouette, sculpting light, magnetic gaze, cinematic tension. Never explicit or vulgar. |
| `/boudoir` | Luxury lingerie-brand or hotel editorial mood. Use delicate fabric, soft diffused light, confident elegant presence, sophisticated not explicit. |
